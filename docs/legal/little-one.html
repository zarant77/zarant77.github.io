Privacy Policy

Little One Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Little One is a single-player arcade game for Android.

Data Collection

Little One does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The game does not use user accounts, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, cloud synchronization, or remote game servers.

Local Data Storage

Little One may store game progress and application settings locally on the user's device. This data may include:

  • high scores and gameplay progress;
  • selected character or unlocked content;
  • sound and music settings;
  • language and control preferences;
  • tutorial completion status.

This information remains on the user's device and is not automatically uploaded or shared.

Permissions

Little One does not require access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, messages, or other personal information.

Android may provide standard platform services required to install, launch, pause, resume, and store local application data.

Advertising and In-App Purchases

Little One does not display third-party advertisements.

Little One does not currently include in-app purchases.

Internet Connection

Little One does not require an internet connection for normal gameplay.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game data remains on the user's device until it is overwritten by the game, cleared through Android settings, or removed when the application is uninstalled.

Little One does not keep a remote copy of game data.

Children's Privacy

Little One does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published on this page.
